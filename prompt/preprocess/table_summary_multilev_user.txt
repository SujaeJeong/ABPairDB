The things that you have to extract from the table is as follows: **title**, **columns**

The table_index is at the very beginning of the table: {table_index}

A **title** is followed by, located between table index and columns. 

**Columns** are followed by, located between title and contents. 
Columns are often written in multiple rows. Thus, it is important to identifying corresponding row spans. 
The column contents are usually bottom-up aligned. 

In some cases, columns are multileveled. 
In given html table format, multilevel columns usually formed wrong. 
Columns in two levels should be matched by their meanings. 
1. Identifying each list of lower-level columns, higher-level columns. 
2. In lower-level column, group repeated columns into groups. 
3. Matching higher-level columns and grouped lower-level columns. 

At the last part of the tables are footnotes. 

# Output format

<table_summary>
<title>
[title]
</title>
<columns>
[columns]
</columns>
</table_summary>
